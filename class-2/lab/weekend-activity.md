### Week 2: Weekend Activity

#### How Is AI Changing Cloud Computing?

AI applications are placing new demands on cloud infrastructure. Large-scale language models and AI agents require significant computing power, memory, networking and energy.

On **12 October 2026**, Microsoft published an architecture overview of **Maia 300**, its new generation of AI infrastructure designed for large-scale AI inference in Microsoft Azure. **Inference** means running a trained AI model to produce responses or predictions.

#### Part A: Read the Article

**Microsoft Azure — “Deep dive into Azure Maia 300 architecture”**

12 October 2026

[Read the article on the Microsoft Azure blog](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/deep-dive-into-azure-maia-300-architecture/).

The article contains technical detail. Start with the introduction, the discussion of power and cooling, and the final section, “What Maia 300 delivers”. You do not need to understand every specification.

#### Part B: Write Your Blog

Write a short **200–300 word blog post** answering:

**How is Artificial Intelligence changing the way cloud infrastructure is designed?**

You may consider:

- Why AI applications need specialised cloud infrastructure.
- The challenges AI creates for computing, networking, memory and energy consumption.
- How Microsoft is addressing these challenges with Maia 300.
- Whether specialised AI hardware will become a normal part of cloud computing.
- What this development could mean for the future of cloud providers such as Microsoft Azure, AWS and Google Cloud.

Choose the aspects you found most interesting; you do not need to cover every question or explain all the technical details. Include your **own opinion** about how AI may change the future of cloud computing. Distinguish Microsoft's claims from your own predictions, and link to the article.

**Length: 200–300 words**, excluding the title and reference link.
