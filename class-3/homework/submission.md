### Week 3: Share Your Work for Feedback

After the Books exercise and testing tutorial, push your lab source, tests and README to your lab repository. Post its link in the module Teams chat with:

- The pytest command and a short summary of passed or failed tests.
- One defect or boundary case your tests check.
- One question or difficulty you would like to discuss in the lab.

Do not upload database files, secrets or your assessed Piazza solution to the chat. This is a practice activity for feedback; it adds no assignment marks.
